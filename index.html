Security & privacy

  • On this device: Meals and photos are encrypted with AES-GCM before saving; your password never leaves the browser as plain text.
  • In the cloud (Supabase): Only an encrypted blob and salt are stored — not readable meal text. Anyone needs your shared password to derive the same vault row.
  • Transport: Use the HTTPS site only. Lock the app when you step away (Settings → Lock).
  • Good habits: Use a strong shared password; don’t share the service_role key from Supabase (only the anon key belongs in the site).
